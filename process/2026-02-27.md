## Concept

For now, our concept is around family language model:

The installation invites visitors to test several physical "cassettes" or "film tapes", each representing a snapshot of a different family's language history over time. When you play one, you hear a story in a familect you can't understand. "Do you remember the last time grandma went woo-hoo? It was a full moot night, we walk by the steeves". It gives the feeling of being an outsider to someone else's intimate world. The neural network / family tree can represent a model that is always incomplete, always growing, never fully connected or the history of family language evolution.

The emotional hook is recognition visitors are meant to leave thinking about their _own_ familect, the words their family invented, the stories behind them and maybe share one.
