# 2026-02-27

We presented our concept and got some things to figure: [2026-02-27](2026-02-27.md).

In families, we often have made up words to describe objects, situations or even a person. Linguists call this a *Familect* or *Familylect* as a contraction of family and dialect. This new language is often a mis-interpretation by children. Sometimes the meaning can be guessed: for example, saying "my computer went bye-bye" to say it turned off. But sometimes, it's based on an intimate story or a shared experience. AI models could be personalised on a specific family dialect. This would mean that a model could not be usable by non-family members. The latter is called Argot, a language used by groups to prevent outsiders from understanding their conversations.
