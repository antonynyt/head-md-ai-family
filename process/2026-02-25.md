## Installation -  Family Language Model

Installation invite people to create their own family language model. As you talk to it, a family tree fills up but instead of people it's new words. It shows a neural network where whole sections are **unpopulated**, and as people add words, new nodes appear. The network will never look like a "complete" AI model. It's a cluster tree of words.

**2 part installation:**

1. You listen/interact with another model but you don't understand anything.
2. You create your family model for other to try.

Lots of family language model on a table, you can try and test a model or you can train your own Family Model. But we should not understand the others family models. Family exclusive AI.

What's the interaction? (voice, text)
